Personal Reflection
After researching AI testing, debugging, and automation, I realized how much software engineering is changing because of artificial intelligence. Before doing this research, I mainly thought of AI as a tool for writing code. However, I learned that AI is also heavily used in testing software, detecting bugs, and automating development processes.
The thing that surprised me most was how much time AI tools can save during debugging and testing. Developers no longer have to manually perform every repetitive task because AI systems can generate tests, analyze logs, and identify potential errors much faster. This can improve productivity and allow engineers to focus on more complex problems.
At the same time, this topic also made me think about the risks of depending too much on automation. One concern I have is that developers may stop improving their problem-solving and debugging skills if they always depend on AI recommendations. I think it is important for software engineers to understand the code they write and not simply trust generated suggestions.
Another important issue is reliability. AI-generated tests or debugging suggestions may sometimes be incorrect. A system may misunderstand business logic or fail to identify hidden problems. Because of this, I believe human supervision is still extremely important. Engineers should review, test, and verify all outputs carefully.
Despite these concerns, I also see many opportunities. AI tools can make software engineering more efficient and reduce repetitive work. They can help beginner developers learn faster and help experienced engineers focus on system design and architecture instead of routine maintenance.
This research changed my opinion about the future of software engineering. I do not think AI will replace developers completely. Instead, I think software engineers will increasingly work together with AI systems. Engineers who understand automation while keeping strong technical and analytical skills will likely have better opportunities in the future.
Personally, I think software engineers should continue learning and adapting. In my own future career, I would like to learn how to use AI tools responsibly while still strengthening my programming, testing, and problem-solving skills.
